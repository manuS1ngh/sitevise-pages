Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Maninderjeet Singh ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you use the Sitevise iOS application ("the App").

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Questions or requests about this policy: sitevise@gmail.com

Information We Collect

Account and Identity

When you create a Sitevise account using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, we receive from Apple or Google:

  • Your email address (or, for Apple private relay users, an Apple-generated relay address)
  • Your display name, if provided
  • A unique account identifier assigned internally by Sitevise

We do not receive or store your Apple ID password or Google account password. Authentication credentials are handled entirely by Apple and Google.

Construction Projects

When you create and manage projects, you may enter:

  • Project name, site address, project manager name and notes
  • Build area and budget information
  • Construction stage names, scheduled dates, status and notes
  • Compliance and certificate records

Financial Records

When you record project costs, you may enter:

  • Expense descriptions, categories and amounts
  • Payment records and status
  • Budget estimates
  • For own-development projects: loan details, development partner names and investment amounts, distribution and repayment records

Financial information is used solely to provide cost-tracking and budgeting features within the App. It is not shared with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

Contacts — Tradespeople and Inspectors

You may add contact details for tradespeople, contractors and inspectors. These may be entered manually or copied from your device's address book. We store:

  • Name, company name, phone number, email address
  • Trade or professional role

Contacts you import are copied into Sitevise's database. The App does not retain ongoing access to your device's address book after import.

You are responsible for ensuring that any third-party personal information entered (such as a contractor's phone number) is provided with appropriate authorisation.

Inspections

When you manage inspections, you may enter:

  • Inspection name, type and scheduled date
  • Inspection result and notes
  • Assigned contact details

Uploaded Files and Documents

You may upload photos, invoices, quotes, receipts, certificates, permits and other construction documents. When you do:

  • File content (the bytes of the photo or document) is stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage.
  • File metadata (file name, file type, storage reference) is stored in our Supabase database.

Files are accessible only by you and any project team members you have explicitly invited.

Team Collaboration

If you invite others to collaborate on a project, Sitevise stores:

  • The email address of each invited person
  • Their assigned role (editor or viewer)
  • Project access records

Invited team members can view or edit the project data you have shared with them according to their role.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create and maintain your Sitevise account
  • Provide the construction project management features of the App
  • Store and retrieve your projects, documents, contacts and financial records
  • Enable team collaboration where you have set it up
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service
  • Process your account deletion request

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or marketing. We do not sell personal information to any third party.

Analytics, Tracking and Advertising

Sitevise does not include analytics SDKs, advertising networks, behavioural tracking or crash-reporting services. No usage data is transmitted to any marketing or advertising platform.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following services to operate Sitevise. Each processes data under our instructions and for no other purpose.

Supabase

Supabase provides our database, authentication and backend services. Your account data, project records, contacts, financial data and file metadata are stored in a Supabase-managed PostgreSQL database hosted on Amazon Web Services in Singapore.

Supabase Privacy Policy: supabase.com/privacy

Cloudflare

Cloudflare R2 object storage holds the content of your uploaded files — photos, invoices, certificates and other documents. Cloudflare operates a global network and file content may be stored on infrastructure outside Australia. Cloudflare does not use stored content for its own commercial purposes.

Cloudflare Privacy Policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates your identity and provides us with your email address (or a private relay address) and name.

Apple Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Google

If you sign in with Google, Google authenticates your identity and provides your email address and name. Sitevise requests only basic identity information from Google — we do not access your Google Drive, Gmail or any other Google service.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

International Data Processing

Your data is primarily stored on Supabase infrastructure in Singapore. Uploaded file content is stored on Cloudflare's global network and may reside on servers located outside Australia. By using Sitevise, you consent to this international processing. We take reasonable steps to ensure our service providers protect personal information to a standard comparable to the Australian Privacy Principles.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including:

  • Row-level security: database access controls ensure each user can access only their own data and data they have been explicitly invited to view.
  • Private file access: uploaded files are not publicly accessible. Access requires short-lived authenticated URLs generated per request.
  • Secure session storage: authentication sessions are stored in your device's secure keychain, not in ordinary app storage.
  • Encrypted transport: all communication uses HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Account Deletion and Data Retention

Requesting account deletion

You can request account deletion at any time from within the App: Settings → Schedule Account Deletion.

Grace period

After you request deletion, your account enters a 35-day grace period. Your account and data remain intact during this time and you can cancel the deletion request from Settings. After the grace period, deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

What is deleted

After the grace period, the following are permanently deleted:

  • Your account and authentication credentials
  • All construction projects, stages, inspections and certificates
  • All uploaded file content
  • All file metadata records
  • All expense and financial records
  • All contacts you have added
  • All project templates
  • All subscription records
  • All pending team invitations

What is retained after deletion

A deletion audit record is retained after your account is removed. This record contains your account identifier (a system-generated UUID), the date of deletion and the number of projects deleted. It does not contain your email address, project data, files or financial records.

This record is retained for operational security and integrity purposes, to maintain an accurate log of account lifecycle events.

Team projects

If you own a project with active team members, your deletion request may be paused until you transfer ownership or remove those members. The App will notify you if this applies.

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Access your information: your data is available within the App at any time. For a formal data export request, contact sitevise@gmail.com.
  • Correct inaccurate information: you can edit project data directly within the App.
  • Request deletion: use Settings → Schedule Account Deletion within the App, or contact us at sitevise@gmail.com.
  • Lodge a complaint: if you believe we have not handled your personal information appropriately, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children's Privacy

Sitevise is designed for professional builders and construction industry participants. It is not intended for use by persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
