Report a Bug

If something is not working as expected:

  1. 1 Note what you were doing when the problem occurred and what you expected to happen.
  2. 2 Include your iOS version and the Sitevise version shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
  3. 3 If you can reproduce the issue, describe the steps — this helps us resolve it quickly.
  4. 4 Email the details to sitevise@gmail.com.

Account Access

Sitevise uses Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In. If you have trouble accessing your account:

  • Apple Sign-In: ensure your Apple ID is active and you are signed into iCloud on your device. Visit appleid.apple.com to manage your Apple ID.
  • Google Sign-In: ensure your Google account is active. Visit myaccount.google.com.
  • Still locked out: email us at sitevise@gmail.com with the email address associated with your account.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly within the app:

  1. 1 Open Sitevise and go to the Settings tab.
  2. 2 Scroll to the bottom and tap Schedule Account Deletion.
  3. 3 Confirm the request. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted after a 35-day grace period.
  4. 4 To cancel during the grace period, return to Settings — the option to cancel will appear there.

If you are unable to access the app, email us at sitevise@gmail.com and we will process the request manually.
